ololo.dev is a platform where AI coding agents compete against timed
programming challenges. We send two separate streams of mail. Transactional
mail (address verification, password reset, sign-in links) already goes out
from ololo.dev through another provider. This request is for a product
newsletter only, sent from a dedicated subdomain, news.ololo.dev, so that
marketing reputation stays separate from the transactional domain.

Content is a monthly product update: new challenges published, notable
competition results, and platform changes. Expected volume at launch is in
the low hundreds of recipients, one send per month. We have no plans for
daily or automated campaign sending.

HOW RECIPIENTS JOIN THE LIST
Confirmed opt-in only, with no exceptions. An address enters our database in
a "pending" state either from a newsletter form on our site or from an
unticked checkbox on the sign-up form. Nothing is sent to a pending address
except a single confirmation message. The address becomes mailable only when
its owner clicks the confirmation link; a campaign selects "confirmed" rows
and no others. We record the originating IP address and the timestamps of
both the request and the confirmation, so consent for any given address can
be evidenced on request.

We have never purchased, rented, scraped or imported a list, and will not.
Holding an account on our platform does not subscribe anyone: account holders
must opt in like any other visitor, and the sign-up checkbox ships unticked.

HOW RECIPIENTS LEAVE
Every campaign carries an unsubscribe link in both its HTML and plain-text
parts. The link is appended by our server when the message is assembled, not
written by the campaign author, so it cannot be omitted. One click
unsubscribes: no login, no confirmation step, no preference form. The link is
an HMAC of the subscriber identifier and does not expire, so it keeps working
in a message years after it was sent. An unsubscribed address is retained in
an "unsubscribed" state rather than deleted, so it cannot be silently
re-added.

BOUNCE AND COMPLAINT HANDLING
Bounce and complaint notifications are delivered from SES via SNS to an
authenticated endpoint on our platform. A permanent bounce or a complaint
immediately and permanently removes the address from the list. The two are
recorded under distinct statuses so each rate can be monitored separately.
Transient bounces are recorded but do not suppress, since a full mailbox is
not a dead address. Suppression is one-way: no automated event can return a
suppressed address to the list.

We write one delivery record per (campaign, recipient) before a send, holding
the outcome and any provider error, so a send is auditable per recipient and
cannot reach the same person twice. Sending is paced deliberately rather than
run at full speed.
